## 🏥 Phase B: Hospital Core (Jan-Jun 2027) - 24 weeks

### B1: Perioperative Care Platform (Jan-Feb 2027) - 8 weeks

**Scope:** Complete perioperative journey (NOT just OR booking)
- [ ] Pre-operative: Surgical consent, safety checklist, patient readiness
- [ ] Intra-operative: OR scheduling, surgical procedures, anesthesia monitoring
- [ ] Post-operative: PACU recovery, discharge criteria, handoff
- [ ] Supporting: CSSD sterilization, equipment traceability, specimen handling

**Engines:** OREngine, SurgicalEngine, AnesthesiaEngine, CssdEngine, PacuEngine, ORReadinessEngine (6 engines)

**Integration:** Consumes Healthcare Kernel (Person/Encounter Aggregate Root)

**Baseline:** [Perioperative Platform Design v1.1 Enterprise](./modules/PERIOPERATIVE_PLATFORM_DESIGN.md)

**Key Features:**
- WHO Surgical Safety Checklist (Sign In/Time Out/Sign Out)
- Database-level OR overlap protection (PostgreSQL EXCLUDE)
- Time-series anesthesia observations (queryable, NOT JSONB)
- Instrument-level CSSD traceability
- Multi-factor OR readiness orchestration
- PACU recovery with Aldrete scores

**Critical Fixes from v1.0:**
1. Healthcare Kernel integration (encounter_id, NOT patient_id)
2. Normalized surgical teams (NOT UUID[])
3. Clinical safety enforcement
4. Event versioning (hos.*.v1)
5. Shadow mode deployment
