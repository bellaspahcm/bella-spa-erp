## SUCCESS DEFINITION

### OS Level Success

**NOT:** "0 human interventions"

**YES:** 
- Human architectural decisions supported with comprehensive evidence
- Construction automated after decisions made
- Verification automated and complete
- Evidence sufficient for reconstruction

### Product Level Success

**YES:** "Approaching 0 unnecessary human interventions"

**Measured by:**
- Throughput (Products per week)
- Conformance (Architecture Guard pass rate)
- Marginal cost (engineering hours per Product)
- Quality consistency (regression protection effectiveness)

## STRATEGIC STATEMENT

> **Bella Factory wins NOT by eliminating human judgment, but by maximizing automation WHERE repetition exists (Product multiplication) while preserving human judgment WHERE it adds value (OS architecture).**

---

## CANONICAL DEFINITION

**Bella Industry OS Factory:**

> A controlled manufacturing system that uses human architectural judgment to establish canonical Industry Operating Systems, then maximizes automation, repeatability, conformance, and evidence when manufacturing Products from those established OSes.

**Core Value Proposition:**

> One canonical Industry OS → Many conformant Products → Marginal manufacturing cost approaching zero → Economic moat through automation leverage.

---

## BOUNDARY GUARD

**When adding Factory capability, ask:**

1. **Does this capability execute repeatedly?**
   - YES → High automation ROI
   - NO → Low priority

2. **Is this architectural judgment or mechanical execution?**
   - Judgment → Human-led
   - Execution → Automation-led

3. **Where is the leverage?**
   - OS creation (5-10 times) → Moderate ROI
   - Product manufacturing (100+ times) → High ROI

4. **What is the marginal benefit?**
   - First use → Setup cost
   - 10th use → ROI neutral
   - 100th use → Strong ROI

**Prioritize capabilities with high repetition and clear mechanical execution.**

---

## ANTI-PATTERNS TO AVOID

❌ **Building inference engine for rare decisions**
- Complexity exceeds benefit
- Human judgment often superior

❌ **Automating strategic architectural judgment**
- Long-term consequences require human expertise
- Low repetition frequency

❌ **Optimizing OS creation throughput over quality**
- OS correctness has multiplier effect on all Products
- Quality > Speed at OS level

❌ **Measuring OS-level work by intervention count**
- Wrong metric
- Encourages automation where judgment better

❌ **Pursuing "fully autonomous" as primary goal**
- Misaligned with economic value
- Over-engineering risk
